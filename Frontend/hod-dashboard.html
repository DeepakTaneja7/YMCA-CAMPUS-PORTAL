HOD Guide

How to manage complaints, upvotes, and SLA deadlines

Managing Complaints

  1. You see all complaints assigned to your department.
  2. SLA Deadlines: High priority = 4 hours, Medium = 24 hours, Low = 48 hours. Unresolved tickets past their deadline appear highlighted in red under Overdue Escalated.
  3. Student Upvotes: Tickets with more 👍 upvotes affect multiple students — sort by Most Upvoted or Escalation Priority to address high-impact problems first.
  4. Use the "Update Status" dropdown to mark issues as Pending, In Progress, Resolved, or Rejected.
  5. Click "Add Remark" to send a live status update visible to students.
ℹ️ Marking a complaint as Resolved automatically clears its overdue flag and records the resolution timestamp.

✏️ Add / Update Remark

Add a remark explaining the action taken, resolution, or reason for rejection. This will be visible to the student.
